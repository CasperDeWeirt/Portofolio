Stylised environment

A stylised environment with structures, made in Blender and Unity.

Project Information

Type: 3D Project

Engine: Unity

Tools: Unity, Blender, Substance Painter

Duration: 2 weeks

Team: Solo

My Role: 3D Artist

About the Project

This environment was created as a final assignment for the "Game art 3" course DAE Howest. The goal was to create a stylised environment with structures, using Blender and Unity. The project focused on creating a visually appealing and immersive environment, using shaders, animations and vfx. It had to have multiple trees, rocks, bushes, grass and buildings/structures. The environment had to be stylised and not realistic, with a focus on creating a unique and interesting world.

Development

The development of this project involved several stages, including concept art, 3D modelling, texturing, and implementation in Unity. I went through multiple cycles of planning, creating assets and then replanning my environment to make it more visually appealing and interesting. From color changes to locations of the structures, I kept switching things up according to feedback and new ideas I got during the development process. The video underneath shows the proces of my iterations.

3D Art & Assets

All 3D assets were created in Blender and textured in Substance Painter. The environment features a variety of stylised trees, rocks, bushes, grass, and buildings/structures. The focus was on creating a cohesive and visually appealing world that fits the stylised aesthetic. All of the assets were made by hand and are unique to this project, with no pre-made assets used. The assets were then imported into Unity and placed in the environment to create a complete scene.

Final Result

The project was completed successfully and met the requirements of the assignment. The final environment is visually appealing and immersive, with a unique stylised aesthetic. It lacked some lighting features, that I tried to correct with post-processing. Due to time constraints, I wasn't able to fix the lighting issues, but that was also a part of the exam. Managing limited time and prioritizing important tasks. I would like to think that by leaving some of the lighting aspects behind, I was able to fix more needed aspects of the environment, like fixing color paletes and placements of nature elements.
