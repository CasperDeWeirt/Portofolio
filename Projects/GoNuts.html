Go Nuts

My first ever game jam game.

Project Information

Type: Game

Engine: Unity

Tools: Unity, Blender

Duration: 32 hours

Team: 3 people

My Role: Game Developer / Designer

About the Project

Go Nuts is a short and chaotic game, where you play as a squirel who fights back against humanity's antics. You decide to take the fight to them and destroy a house!

This was my first ever game jam, this was for the DAE Howest course "Game Design 2". Its a very simple game but it taught me a lot about teamwork and planning.

My Role

In this project I was part of the game design team and I was one of the two programmers. I did a lot of the movement code and also worked on making the interactable objects move when the squirrel interacted with them. I also animated the squirrels movement.

  • Gameplay programming
  • Level design
  • Game design

Gameplay

The gameplay is pretty simple, you rurn around colliding with objects and breaking things. Breaking things gives you points and increases your end score. The player also has a checklist of special task that they must complete to get an even bigger score and get a secret ending.

Final Result

The end project is nothing special and it's very simple, but I'm still proud of the small game I made with my team in my first game jam. We all have to start somewhere. In my case, it was this silly little game.
