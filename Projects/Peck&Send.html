Peck&Send

Fun game to play with friends. Group project for DAE Howest.

Project Information

Type: Game

Engine: Unity

Tools: Unity, Blender

Duration: 11 weeks - 20 hours per week (220 hours)

Team: 6 people

My Role: Game designer / Programmer

About the Project

Welcome to PeckNSend - the most trusted mail delivery company in Twin Beaks! Here, at PeckNSend, we proudly perform imPECKable task of delivering mail across our charming little town. In the fast-paced 2-4 employee shift, you must catch incoming mail, deliver it to the correct mailboxes, and push your colleagues aside to climb the career ladder of your dreams!

Peck&Send is a fun and quick game to play with friends and family. Its a simple and chaotic game to enjoy and replay over and over. This game was made for the "Game projects" course at DAE Howest. It was a group project with 6 people in total.

My Role

In this project I was one of three programmers in our group. I was responsible for implementing the art or our amazing artists into the game, as well as making it work properly. The code i programmed was mainly focused on the spawning patterns of mail and road items, the background and the mailboxes. I also helped the other programmers with the hitboxes and bugfixes in the menus.

  • Gameplay programming
  • Level design
  • Game design

Development

Development of this project was a lot of fun. We had a lot of freedom in the design and development of the game, which allowed us to be creative and try new things. It took around 11 weeks, where each week we had to work around 20 hours on the project. Each week we had to present our progress to the teachers, which was a great way to get feedback and improve the game. Not every week went as smooth as we wanted, but we managed to overcome the challenges and finish the project on time. So I'd liked to call this game a success!

Gameplay

PECKNSEND is a multiplayer delivery game where players take on the role of flying birds tasked with delivering mail around a city. Players must navigate the environment while carrying different types of envelopes, including normal, heavy and fragile packages. Different mailboxes provide delivery targets, while other players collisions make navigating the city more challenging. Players earn points through successful deliveries and compete against each other. The bird with the most deliveries becomes the employee of the day.

Final Result

The final result is a working and also fun looking game. I'm very happy with how this ended up, and I learned a lot from this project. I also had the pleasure of working with an amazing team of people, and I hope to work with them again in the future.
